JAWS Project

McKibben muscle jaw, Soft Robotics Clinic, Rowan University, Fall 2026

This page has the links and files for our jaw project. Students use these during the jaw testing and for the muscle tests in Phase 1. Everything runs in a web browser, so there is nothing to install and no sign-in.

Pages

LinkWhat it does
ScoreboardLive results for the projector. Ranks teams by strongest bite, by muscle orientation, and by calculated vs. measured force. Students can open it on their phones too.
Team stationOne per team, on a laptop in Chrome or Edge. Connects to the load cell over USB and saves the peak force of every squeeze.
Jaw force calculatorCalculates the predicted bite force for each orientation from the jaw measurements, with the uncertainty, the best muscle angle, and every step of the math shown. It can load a team's squeezes from the scoreboard and send the calculated forces back to it.
Force vs. pressureGraphs muscle force against pressure at each contraction and fits F = P(c₁ − c₂ε). Paste in the output of the force/pressure logger.
Muscle pressureGraphs pressure over time from the pressure logger and gives the peak pressure and leak rate.

The load cell sketches need the "HX711 Arduino Library" by Bogdan Necula, and the pressure logger needs the "Adafruit MPRLS Library". Both are in the Arduino Library Manager.

Phase 1: force vs. pressure test

  1. Hang the muscle from the load cell, with the syringe, tee and pressure sensor on the same air line.
  2. Upload muscle_force_pressure.ino and open the Serial Monitor (115200 baud, Newline).
  3. With the muscle deflated, type t to zero both sensors.
  4. Set the muscle length with the turnbuckle, measure how much it has shortened, and type c and the number in mm (for example c12).
  5. Push the syringe up through about six pressures and type h at each one. Then come back down through a few.
  6. Do the same at two or more other lengths. Type n before repeating a trial.
  7. Paste everything from the Serial Monitor into the force vs. pressure page and enter the muscle's length, diameter and braid turns.

Phase 1: pressure and leak test

  1. Upload muscle_pressure_logger.ino to the UNO R4. The Serial Plotter shows the pressure live.
  2. In the Serial Monitor, type r, then push and hold the syringe. It records for 20 seconds.
  3. Copy the line that starts with DATA and paste it into the muscle pressure page. Paste more DATA lines to average several tests.
  4. Keep it under 9 psi so the sensor doesn't get damaged.
