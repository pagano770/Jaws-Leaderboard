Jaw force app

Students, sign in with their Rowan account

Muscle force, torque (cross product), moment balance, uncertainty from partial derivatives, best muscle angle, force vs. pressure and pressure calibration. "Show the math" prints every step with the team's numbers. It imports squeezes from the leaderboard.

Characterizing the muscle (Phase 1)

Measures how much force the muscle makes at each pressure and length, so the jaw model can use a fitted formula in place of a = −1.6 N/mm.

Force vs. pressure tool

Any browser

Paste the logger's STEP lines. It plots force against pressure at each contraction, fits F = P(c₁ − c₂ε), and reports R², error, efficiency vs. the ideal muscle, repeatability and hysteresis, with every calculation shown.

Open the tool

Force–pressure logger

Arduino Uno + HX711 + 0–80 psi sensor on A0

Reads pressure and force together. Type t to zero, c12 to set the contraction in mm, n for a new trial, and h at each syringe step to save a 2-second average.

View or download

Running the muscle test

  1. Hang the muscle from the load cell with the syringe, tee and pressure sensor on one air line. Upload the logger and open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud, "Newline".
  2. With the muscle deflated and nothing pulling, send t.
  3. Set the length with the turnbuckle, measure how much the muscle has shortened, and send c plus that number in mm.
  4. Step the syringe up through about six pressures, sending h at each one, then come back down through a few.
  5. Repeat for at least two more lengths, and send n before each repeat trial.
  6. Paste the Serial Monitor output into the Force vs. pressure tool and enter the muscle's L₀, D₀ and braid turns.
