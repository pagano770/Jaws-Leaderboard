Muscle Characterization

Turns your blocked-force tests into a formula for the muscle's force, F = P(c₁ − c₂ε) + c₀, then uses it to predict the force at any pressure and contraction. Data comes from the force–pressure logger; the steps are in the build and test guide. Your data and inputs are kept in this browser.

4. Use this model in the jaw calculator

Saves c₁, c₂, c₀ and L₀ so the jaw force calculator can predict muscle force from pressure, On another computer, copy the model code and paste it there.

Each contraction

Slope is a straight line through that contraction's points alone, so it shows how much force each psi adds at that length. Repeatability compares that slope between trials (target ≤ 10%). Hysteresis compares force on the way down with force on the way up at the same pressure.
