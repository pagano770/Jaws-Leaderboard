Each contraction

Slope is a straight line through that contraction's points alone, so it shows how much force each kPa adds at that length. Repeatability compares that slope between trials (target ≤ 10%). Hysteresis compares force on the way down with force on the way up at the same pressure.

How the numbers were calculated

Class model a at one pressure

The hanging-mass model F = a(x − b) is the fitted formula at one fixed pressure: a = −c₂P ÷ L₀ and b = c₁L₀ ÷ c₂ (free contraction). Pick a pressure to compare with a = −1.6 N/mm.
